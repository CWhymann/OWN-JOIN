About this project

Join is an educational project developed during a web development bootcamp and not a commercial service. This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you use it.

Which data we process

When you sign up, we process your name, your email address and your password. The password is stored only as a hash by our authentication provider, we cannot read it. Contacts and tasks you create (for example names, email addresses, phone numbers, task titles, descriptions and due dates) are stored in our database so that you can use the app. When you open the website, your browser transmits technical data such as your IP address to the hosting provider.

Guest login

You can try Join with the guest login without providing personal data. The guest account is shared by all visitors, so please do not enter real personal data or data of third parties there.

Purpose and legal basis

We process your data to provide the functions of Join (Art. 6 (1) b GDPR) and to operate and secure the website (Art. 6 (1) f GDPR).

Service providers

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Authentication and the database are provided by Supabase. These providers process data on our behalf and may process it outside the European Union. Please also refer to the privacy information of these providers.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and no external fonts or third-party scripts are loaded. To keep you signed in, your browser stores a session token in its local storage. It is removed when you log out.

Storage period

We store your data as long as your account exists. On request, we delete your account and the data connected to it.
